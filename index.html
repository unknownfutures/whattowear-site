The idea

Fashion is a beautiful way to arrive.

Every city dresses in its own way. The fabric that suits the humidity. The colours people reach for in winter. The market where a weaver still sells the cloth her family has made for generations. The tailor everyone from here knows and no guidebook mentions.

whattowear.in is a carefully curated guide to finding those things. Find the textile. Find the shop. Learn how people wear what they wear, and bring a little of it home.

Then share it. Plan your itinerary with friends and let them add the places they loved. Stay connected with the people you enjoyed along the way: the stylist who knew exactly what you needed, the shop owner who made you feel at home.

Keep your own sense of style, and elevate each other.

Hi, I'm Stephanie. I'm from Curaçao, I live in Atlanta, and my family is spread across four continents. My younger sister Mo and I are always arriving somewhere new.

Every time, it's the same questions. What do people wear here? Where do they shop? Who should we meet? The best answers never came from a search. They came from people: a stylist, a shop owner, a friend of a friend.

whattowear.in is how we're gathering those answers, and the people behind them. We'll share what we find as we go: the textiles, the shops, the outfits that worked and the ones that didn't.
